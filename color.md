## Recommended Brand Usage

- **Primary background:** `#010612`
- **Secondary background:** `#051528`
- **Technology / primary accent:** `#08B8F8`
- **Secondary blue:** `#0878F8`
- **Warm accent:** `#F87818`
- **Main text:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Metallic details:** `#777675` and `#ACCBD0`

### Usage Guidelines

1. Use deep navy as the dominant background color.
2. Use cyan and electric blue for circuit graphics, links, icons, and technology-focused details.
3. Use orange selectively for emphasis, highlights, and calls to action.
4. Use white for primary text to maintain strong contrast against dark backgrounds.
5. Reserve silver tones for metallic effects, borders, and secondary details.
6. Avoid using blue and orange at equal intensity everywhere; keep one as the primary accent and the other for emphasis.
